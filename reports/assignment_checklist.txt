ASSIGNMENT CHECKLIST - saved October 23, 2026 experiment

1. GitHub repository
   https://github.com/rovinako/used-car-value-retention

2. Source code
   src/collect_data.py, src/prepare_data.py, src/train_models.py
   run_project.py runs the saved-data pipeline.
   notebooks/project_walkthrough.ipynb explains each step.

3. Two independently operated listing sources
   CarGurus: 1,824 initially parsed rows; 887 valid unique cars before integration.
   iSeeCars: 1,080 initially parsed rows; 736 valid unique cars before integration.
   data/raw/ contains a separate CSV for each source, plus collection URLs/timestamps.
   Repeated search-page appearances are removed rather than counted as new cars.

4. Minimum 1,000 after preprocessing and deduplication
   1,325 final cars; unique VINs; 884 CarGurus and 441 iSeeCars records.
   The per-source 500 minimum applies before integration and is satisfied by both.
   See data/processed/integrated_cars.csv and data_checks.json.

5. Make/model limits
   Three makes, three models each, nine model families total.
   Toyota: Camry, Corolla, RAV4.
   Honda: Accord, Civic, CR-V.
   Ford: Fusion, Escape, Explorer.

6. Historical MSRP
   783 trim-level reference rows in data/raw/msrp.csv.
   Every retained car has a matching make, model, model year and trim MSRP.
   Source: CarGurus Research. Unclear matches are excluded, not guessed.
   Optional packages and destination-charge differences are limitations.

7. At least ten original attributes
   VIN, listing ID, make, model, year, trim, price, mileage, body type, color,
   listing title and location, plus additional source-specific attributes.
   See data/DATA_DICTIONARY.md.

8. Preprocessing and attribute statistics
   Field-name/unit standardization, validity checks, VIN deduplication, MSRP joins.
   See cleaning_steps.csv, attribute_statistics.csv and missing_values.csv.

9. Feature engineering and high-cardinality encoding
   Vehicle age, miles per year (exploration), and two equipment-description flags:
   mentions_leather and mentions_navigation. These use short listing equipment
   descriptions, not full seller-written paragraphs. Missing text stays unknown.
   Trim and city are one-hot encoded with infrequent categories grouped (<10).
   Encoding and imputation are fitted within training folds.

10. Three regression models and multiple metrics
    Ridge regression, random forest, gradient boosting.
    Same 80/20 train/test split; 3-fold training cross-validation; seed 42.
    MAE, RMSE and R-squared, plus a mean-prediction baseline.
    See model_results.csv and experiment_summary.json.

11. Depreciation analysis
    Permutation importance, ridge coefficients, descriptive correlations and
    partial-dependence plots. No causal claims.

12. Visualizations
    reports/figures/: distributions, model/source counts, model comparison,
    feature ranking, age/mileage scatter plots and partial dependence.

13. Final report
    reports/final_report.pdf (5 pages) and editable plain-text copy.
    Covers project goal, collection, integration, feature engineering, models,
    results, depreciation, visualizations, conclusion, lessons and challenges.
    AI usage is explicitly disclosed in section 10 and in README.md.

14. Verification
    python src/check_project.py checks counts, coverage, duplicate VINs, MSRP,
    target arithmetic, text features, split separation, metrics and figures.
